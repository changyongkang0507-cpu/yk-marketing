Quick answers

FAQ, because
confirm got questions.

Is the ad budget included in the monthly package?

No. Standard includes management of 1 basic Meta Ads campaign and Premium includes 2, but the money spent on Meta is paid separately by the client.

Can I add TikTok, Xiaohongshu or YouTube?

Yes. Extra basic account setup is RM50/platform. Additional full platform management is RM250/month/platform and uses your existing package creatives within a defined scope. Complex verification and new creative production are extra.

Will you handle Shopee / Lazada customer service and delivery?

Our regular e-commerce packages cover defined store upkeep and marketing, not full-day customer service, warehouse operations, parcel packing or disputes. Full operations can be quoted separately.

Do you charge a percentage of online sales?

No. We use agreed fixed project fees, monthly retainers or hourly live-selling rates, not sales commissions.

How many revisions are included?

Up to two reasonable rounds per creative within the agreed brief. New directions, additional shooting, major reworks and urgent requests are quoted separately.

Can you arrange a booth, sampling event or roadshow?

Yes — subject to feasibility, vendors, permits and location. Event planning and activation are always quoted after discussing scope, staffing, logistics and dates.
